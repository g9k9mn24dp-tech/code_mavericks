Your instincts are about to become your worst enemy. Don't Do That! is a fast-paced reaction game where you're given an instruction — and your job is to do the exact opposite.

Sounds easy? It isn't. As the pressure builds, you'll need quick thinking, sharp reactions and enough self-control to ignore what the game is telling you.
