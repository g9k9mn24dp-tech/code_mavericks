Delete Your Literally Illiterate Account

This page explains how users of Literally Illiterate, developed by Code Mavericks, can request deletion of their account and associated data.

Delete your account in the app

You can delete your account directly from the Literally Illiterate app. Sign in to your account, open your account or profile settings, and select the option to delete your account.

If you cannot access the app

If you are unable to access your account, you can contact Code Mavericks and request account deletion. Please use the contact details provided on the Code Mavericks website and identify the email address associated with your Literally Illiterate account.

What is deleted

When your account is deleted, the account and associated personal data stored for the operation of Literally Illiterate will be deleted, except for information that must be retained where required by law, security, fraud prevention, or other legitimate legal obligations.
