Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Code Mavericks collects, uses and protects information when you use our apps, games, website and support services.

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you provide directly to us, including:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Support messages
  • Feedback you send to us
  • Purchase-related information or identifiers
  • Account or profile information where applicable

We do not directly store payment card or banking details.

How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Provide and operate our apps, games and website
  • Respond to customer support requests
  • Help resolve technical or account problems
  • Process and investigate purchase-related issues
  • Improve gameplay, features and user experience
  • Prevent fraud, abuse or misuse of our services
  • Communicate with users about support or service matters

In-App Purchases

Purchases made inside our iOS apps are processed through Apple and the App Store. Code Mavericks does not receive or store your full payment card details from Apple.

If you contact us about an in-app purchase, we may ask for information such as the item purchased, approximate purchase date, app version or other details needed to investigate the issue.

Website Payments

Some purchases made through the Code Mavericks website may be processed by third-party payment providers such as Stripe.

These payment providers may process personal information under their own privacy policies and terms. Code Mavericks does not store full payment card details.

Cookies and Local Storage

Our website may use essential cookies or local storage for site functionality, preferences, checkout processes and other necessary features.

If optional analytics or advertising technologies are introduced in the future, we will update our privacy information and consent experience as required.

App Data and Progress

Some Code Mavericks apps may store information related to gameplay, progress, scores, achievements, preferences, unlocks or account features.

This information may be stored locally on your device or through services used by the app where applicable.

Support Information

When you contact us for support, we may use the information you provide to investigate and respond to your request.

Please do not send passwords, full payment card details, banking details or other unnecessary sensitive financial information.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers only where necessary to operate our services, process transactions, provide technical functionality, or comply with legal obligations.

Data Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected, including providing services, responding to support requests, meeting legal obligations and resolving disputes.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including rights to request access, correction or deletion of certain information.

You can contact us using the email address below if you have a privacy request.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in a way that is prohibited by applicable law.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us inappropriately, please contact us so we can investigate.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
