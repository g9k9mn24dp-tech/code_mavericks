Gameplay Help

Don't Do That is a reverse-logic arcade game where the goal is often to choose the wrong answer, break the rule, or react in the opposite way.

If something goes wrong, tell us which game mode you were playing and what happened.

Game modes include Classic, Chaos Grid, Simon Says... Don't, Memory Mayhem, Rule Flip, Reverse Trivia, Endless Chaos, multiplayer modes and Boss Battles.

Account & Progress Help

Contact us if you experience problems with:

  • XP or level progression
  • Daily or weekly missions
  • Chaos Road rewards
  • Bomb Crew unlocks
  • Achievements
  • Saved progress
  • Profile or account features

Technical Support

If the app freezes, crashes, becomes unresponsive, or a game mode does not work correctly, please contact us with as much detail as possible.

Including your device model, app version, and a screenshot can help us investigate more quickly.
