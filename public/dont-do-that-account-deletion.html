Don't Do That - Account Deletion

Users of Don't Do That can request deletion of their account and associated data.

You can delete your account directly inside the Don't Do That app by going to Profile and selecting Delete My Account.

If you are unable to access the app, you can request account deletion by emailing: codemavericks2706@outlook.com

Please include the email address associated with your Don't Do That account.

When your account is deleted, associated account data such as saved progress, profile information and coin balance will be deleted, except for information that may need to be retained for legal, fraud-prevention, tax or transaction-record purposes.

Account deletion requests will be processed as soon as reasonably possible.
